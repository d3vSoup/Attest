How it works
01
Payment fails
A UPI reject, card decline, or gateway timeout hits the Attest pipeline. Every event is ingested with full metadata — amount, channel, time, merchant.
02
AI classifies
XGBoost with SHAP explainability routes the failure: fraud, gateway error, 3DS auth failure, or insufficient funds. The right recovery action fires automatically.
03
Anomalies flagged
Isolation Forest runs before the classifier. Velocity spikes, off-hours patterns, amount outliers — caught before they become chargebacks. R₀ 0.035 — contained.
04
Proof anchored
Every AI decision is SHA-256 hashed, Merkle-batched, and anchored on Polygon Amoy. Any post-hoc alteration is immediately detectable. 100% catch rate.
